Identifying unserved needs, friction points, and root cause analysis.

Step 3 — Customer Research Strategy
Detailed 21-point Customer Research Strategy alongside VoC Evidence.

Step 4 — Problem Definition
Formal problem statements mapping the core issues to be solved.

Step 4 — Customer Personas & JTBD (parallel to Step 4)
Target user archetypes, pain points, motivations & Jobs-to-be-Done.

Step 5 — Opportunity Assessment
Evaluating potential business value, technical feasibility, and market risk.

Step 6 — Strategy Canvas Studio
Mission, vision, target market positioning, and core value proposition.

Step 6 — Product Strategy Doc (parallel to Step 6)
Master synthesis of vision, positioning, market analysis & OKRs.

Step 6 — Strategic Outcomes Hub (parallel to Step 6)
Unified dashboard for North Star metrics, OKRs, and Performance Reports.

Step 7 — Opportunity Prioritization Matrix
Scoring framework evaluating Reach, Impact, Confidence & Effort.

Step 8 — Product Roadmap Document
Now-Next-Later theme roadmap and milestone releases schedule.

Step 9 — Solution Design & Wireframes
Ideation, user flows, architecture sketches, and UI wireframes.

Step 10 — Validation & Experiments
Fake-door tests, A/B hypotheses, and prototype user testing results.

Step 10 — Quality Management Plan (parallel to Step 10)
Quality standards, QA/QC audit protocols, inspection checklists, and metrics.

Step 11 — Product Requirements Doc
Problem statements, user stories, acceptance criteria, & scope boundaries.

Step 12 — Release Launch Checklist
Go-to-market readiness, QA sign-off, security audit & press.
