1. Initiation
Business Case – justifies why the project should happen (problem, opportunity, ROI)
Project Charter – formally authorizes the project; defines objectives, scope, sponsor, and PM authority
Stakeholder Register – list of stakeholders, their interests, influence, and communication needs
Feasibility Study (if applicable) – technical/financial/operational viability


Planning
Project Management Plan – the master document tying together all sub-plans
Scope Statement / Scope of Work (SOW) – defines what's in and out of scope
Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) – breaks deliverables into manageable tasks
Project Schedule / Gantt Chart – timeline, milestones, dependencies
Budget / Cost Baseline – estimated costs, funding sources
Risk Register – identified risks, likelihood, impact, mitigation plans
Communication Plan – who gets what info, how often, via which channel
Resource Plan – team roles, responsibilities (often a RACI matrix), and resource allocation
Quality Management Plan – standards and acceptance criteria
Procurement Plan (if using vendors/contractors)
Change Management Plan – process for handling scope/schedule/budget changes


Execution
Status Reports (weekly/biweekly) – progress against plan
Meeting Minutes – decisions and action items from standups, reviews, etc.
Issue Log – tracks problems as they arise and resolution status
Action Item Tracker
Change Request Log – documents requested changes and approvals
Deliverable Sign-off Sheets – client/stakeholder acceptance of completed work|


Monitoring & Control
Performance Reports (e.g., Earned Value Analysis, burn-down/burn-up charts)
Risk & Issue Updates – living documents updated regularly
Budget Tracking / Variance Reports
Quality Audit Reports


Closure
Project Closure Report – confirms deliverables met, formally ends project
Lessons Learned Document – what worked, what didn't, recommendations
Final Handover Document – transitions deliverables/ops to the business
Post-Implementation Review – evaluates outcomes vs. original goals
