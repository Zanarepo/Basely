1. Executive Summary
The Sellytics Inventory Platform project aims to design, develop, and deploy a cloud-based inventory management solution that enables small and medium-sized businesses to efficiently manage inventory, suppliers, customers, purchases, sales, and business analytics while leveraging artificial intelligence to improve operational decision-making.
The project will deliver an MVP focused on core inventory operations, followed by incremental releases introducing reporting, notifications, and AI-powered capabilities.

2. Business Case
Many SMEs continue to manage inventory using spreadsheets or disconnected software, leading to:
Inventory inaccuracies
Stock shortages
Overstocking
Poor purchasing decisions
Limited operational visibility
The project seeks to address these issues through an affordable, scalable SaaS platform.

3. Project Objectives
The project aims to:
Deliver a cloud-based inventory management platform.
Reduce inventory inaccuracies.
Improve operational efficiency.
Enable real-time inventory visibility.
Introduce AI-powered inventory insights.
Provide a scalable architecture for future growth.
